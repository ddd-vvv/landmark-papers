* Neurophysiological phenomenon where a nerve fiber exhibits a transient increase in excitability following a brief, subthreshold electrical stimulus.
* How it works:
	* Subthreshold Depolarization: The initial stimulus sligthly charges the nerve membrane, bringing it closer to its firing threshold.
	* Ion Channel Activation: The slight change in voltage opens a small fraction of non-classical, voltage-dependent sodium ($\text{Na}^+$) channels that act close to the resting potential.
	* Summation Window: Because these channels open but do not immediately inactivate, a second subthreshold pulse deliverd shortly after (whithin a fracton of a millisecond) can "add" to the first one, succesfully trigerring an action potential.
