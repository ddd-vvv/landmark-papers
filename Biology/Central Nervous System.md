# Content
* The central nervous system (CNS) is the part of the [[Nervous System | nervous system]] consisting primarily of the [[Brain | brain]] and the [[Spinal cord | spinal cord]].
* The [[Retina | retina]] is also technically part of the CNS.
* The brain integrates the received information and coordintes and influences the activity of all parts of the bodies of  [[Bilaterally symmetric and triploblastic animals | bilaterally symmetric and triploblastic animals]] (all multicellular animals except sponges and diploblats).
* Is a structure composed of [[Nervous tissue | nervous tissue]] positioned along the [[Rostral (nose end) to causal (tail end) | rostal (nose end) to causal (tail end)]] axis of the body and may have and elarged section at the rostral end which is a brain.
