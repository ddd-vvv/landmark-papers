* In[[Kantian philosophy]], the **thing-in-itself** is the status of [[Objects | objects]] as they are, independent of representation and observation.
* The concept of the **thing-in-itself** was introduced by the German philosopher [[Immanuel Kant]].
* Since then it has been met with controversy among later philosophers.
* Closely related to [[Immanuel Kant | Kant's]] concept of [[Noumena | noumena]] of the objects of inquiry, as opposed to [[Phenomena | phenomena]], its manifestations.
